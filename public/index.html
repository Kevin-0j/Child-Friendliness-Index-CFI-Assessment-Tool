The Problem With Modern Design

Why we need a dedicated standard for children.

1

Adult-First By Default

Buildings are made for grown-ups. Counters are out of reach, seating doesn't fit, and getting around is confusing for a child.

2

The Unseen Hazards

Standard codes check for structural safety but miss the details. A safe space for you might be full of sharp edges and blind spots for a toddler.

3

Our Standard

We built the Child Friendly Index to fix this. We evaluate your space and give you the exact steps needed to make it truly welcoming for families.

Why evaluate
your space?

Understanding how children experience your building is the very first step to creating a better environment for everyone.

01.

Attract Families

Families go where they feel safe and comfortable. When you prioritize child-friendly design, you naturally become their first choice.

02.

Reduce Risk

Proactively identifying hidden hazards protects children from injuries and protects your business from liability.

03.

Boost Engagement

Children who are engaged and stimulated are happier. Happy children mean relaxed parents who stay longer and return often.

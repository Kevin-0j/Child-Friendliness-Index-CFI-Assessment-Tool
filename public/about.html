Our Guiding Principle

Designing Spaces That Welcome Tomorrow.

We don't merely assess buildings. We are part of a pan-African movement to reclaim urban spaces for children, recognizing that a city fit for a child is a city fit for everyone.

"Access to safe, inclusive public space is directly linked to children's health, cognitive development, learning and social ties. It is not an amenity; it is a fundamental child right."

— World Health Organization (WHO)

"The sight of children active and visible in public space is a sign not just of their own wellbeing, but of a healthy, liveable urban environment. The child is the indicator species for cities."

— Enrique Peñalosa

Strategic Alignment

Aligned with global benchmarks, localized for African cities.

Inspired by the UNICEF Child Friendly Cities Initiative and WHO Urban Health Guidelines, we evaluate high-density developments through a rigorous lens: recognizing that high-rise vertical living in Nairobi presents unique opportunities and distinct safety challenges.

Our Vision

Every African child deserves an urban habitat that protects their curiosity, guarantees physical safety, and connects them with peers and nature—whether on the ground or 20 stories in the air.

Our Methodology

We don't hand developers an abstract checklist. We provide evidence-based scoring across 5 established domains—Built Environment, Physical Safety, Play Infrastructure, Environmental Wellness, and Governance—paired with pragmatic retrofit engineering roadmaps.

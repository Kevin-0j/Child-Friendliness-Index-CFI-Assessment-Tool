Our philosophy.

We don't just assess buildings. We are part of a global movement to reclaim spaces for the next generation.

"Access to safe, inclusive public space is directly linked to children's health, development, learning and social ties and is a child's right."

— World Health Organization (WHO)

"The sight of children being active and visible in public space is a sign not just of their own wellbeing, but of a generally healthy, liveable urban environment. The child is an indicator species for cities."

— Enrique Peñalosa

Aligned with a global framework.

Inspired by the UNICEF Child Friendly Cities Initiative and WHO guidelines, we recognize that the built environment directly shapes a child's health and future.

Our Vision

We believe every space should be a place where children thrive. We are here to translate these global ideals into a tangible, measurable standard for every building and environment.

Our Mission

We give you the exact steps needed to make your property safe, engaging, and welcoming for families. We never just hand you a checklist. We work with you to build a space that kids actually love, proving that prioritizing children makes the entire community stronger.
